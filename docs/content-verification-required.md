# Content verification required

The new core service pages intentionally omit unverified proof and fixed commercial claims. Before any of the following are published, retain source evidence and approval:

- Client, project, business, team, platform, or partner counts
- Managed spend, revenue, ROAS, CAC, conversion, traffic, or ranking results
- Client logos, testimonials, previous employers, certifications, and biographies
- Fixed delivery periods, output quotas, minimum media budgets, or public prices
- Guaranteed ranking, lead, sales, revenue, or advertising outcomes

Legacy pages may still contain earlier claims. They should be audited separately before their content is migrated into the new architecture.
